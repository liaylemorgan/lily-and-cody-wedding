Getting there

From Hull, follow the A1033 towards Hedon and Withernsea, continuing through Thorngumbald, Keyingham, Ottringham and Winestead. Dunedin is on Westgate as you enter Patrington.

The venue also notes that East Yorkshire buses 75 and X7 stop in Patrington nearby.

Please follow any wedding-day parking signage or staff directions when you arrive.
