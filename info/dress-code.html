Dress Code

Come dressed to celebrate

The important bit

Please avoid sage green, white, cream and silver clothing. These colours are being kept for the wedding party, so consider them firmly off the guest menu.

Silver jewellery and accessories are absolutely fine — this restriction is about clothing, not your earrings, necklace, watch, bag or other finishing touches.

A particularly important note about white

If your outfit has you preparing a legal submission explaining why it is “ivory”, “champagne”, “off-white”, “mostly patterned” or “only a little bridal”, you already know the answer.

We would strongly recommend choosing something else. White has a surprisingly poor survival rate around wedding-day drinks, and anyone determined to test the rule may discover that their outfit is not the only thing being given the boot.

In short: if the clothing could reasonably be mistaken for white, cream, sage green or silver in a photo, leave it on the hanger. Silver jewellery and accessories get a free pass. There are plenty of other colours in the world — please pick one of them.
