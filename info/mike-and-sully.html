Their wedding-day arrangements

Mike & Sully will be cared for by their dog walker during the wedding.

Please do not approach, stroke or feed them unless you already have an established relationship with them and their walker is happy for you to do so.

A little extra note about Mike:

Mike can feel nervous around unfamiliar people and may react if he becomes frightened, overwhelmed or startled.

Because of this, we kindly ask guests who do not already know him to give him plenty of space and allow his walker to manage any introductions.

These arrangements are not there to make him seem unfriendly — they are simply the best way to keep Mike relaxed, keep guests comfortable, and make sure everyone can enjoy the day safely.

Thank you

They are both very special members of the family, and we really appreciate everyone helping us make sure their wedding day is just as calm and enjoyable as ours.
