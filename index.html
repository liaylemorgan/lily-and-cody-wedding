Frequently Asked Questions

The useful bits, with a little attitude
What time should I arrive?

Your personalised invitation has your arrival time. Please follow that one—this wedding does not operate an “arrive early for a free upgrade” policy.

Can I bring my children?

Only children specifically named on your invitation are invited. If their name is not there, they are not on the guest list. Tiny humans are still humans.

Can I wear patterned white?

Please avoid white, cream and sage green. If you’re preparing a courtroom defence for your outfit, perhaps choose another one.

What if my dietary requirements change?

Please update your RSVP if something changes. Allergies and dietary needs matter; discovering you dislike potatoes during the wedding breakfast is slightly less helpful.

Do I have to dance?

Bring your enthusiasm. Coordination is optional, and we make no promises about anyone’s uncle.

Can I request a song?

You may suggest one. A suggestion is not a legally binding contract, especially if it lasts nine minutes.

Can I propose at the wedding?

We admire your commitment to saving money on venue hire, but please save your big moment for another day.

Can I make an impromptu speech?

Please leave speeches to the people asked to give them. Your podcast debut can wait.

Can I pay cash at the bar?

No. The bar is cashless, so please bring a debit or credit card. Your charming personality is not accepted as legal tender.

I’ve lost my invitation code. What do I do?

Get in touch and we’ll help. Please don’t borrow someone else’s—identity theft is an unusual wedding gift.

Can I ask something that’s already answered on here?

You can, but please have a little scroll first. The answers are here, wearing their best fonts.

Where can I stay?

Accommodation recommendations and local taxi information will be added here before invitations are sent.
